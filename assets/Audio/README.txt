Stormtide recordings supplied for Raid Roulette:
Thunder1.wav, ThunderReplacement.wav, HeavyRain.wav.
Build.ps1 copies these into the client output Audio folder automatically.
The included files use 16-bit PCM WAV, stereo 32 kHz.
HeavyRain.wav is a 30-second loop. Thunder2 and Thunder3 are retired.
Stormtide crossfades the native rain into HeavyRain.wav and selects from the
two thunder recordings. Keep Audio beside RaidRoulette.Testing.dll at runtime.
