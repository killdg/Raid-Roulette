# Raid Roulette event overview

**Boss Run Down** — A random boss is hunting you. Keep moving or prepare an ambush before the hunter reaches you.

**Boss Convention** — Several bosses have gathered on the same map. Expect their guards and some very unfriendly company.

**Locked Loot** — Locked rooms hide premium loot. Bring your keys and make room for one to three valuable finds behind each keyed door.


**Raider Assault** — Raiders replace the usual opposition. Expect military equipment and a tougher fight.

**Cultist Invasion** — Cultists have taken over the raid. Watch the shadows and keep your distance from their knives.

**Scav Concentrate** — Scavs gather into roaming groups. Finding one may mean finding the rest of his friends.

**PMC Sweep** — USEC and BEAR groups replace the usual opposition. Expect a raid full of armed contractors.

**Get Handy** — Scavs and PMCs fight with handguns. Flashbangs are their only carried grenades.

**I Have A Shot** — Scavs and PMCs carry shotguns. Watch close corners and expect flashbangs.

**Marksman** — Scavs and PMCs carry sniper rifles. Long sightlines are dangerous; flashbangs can still catch you off guard.

**Explosive Scav Units** — Scavs carry handguns and grenade launchers. A quiet street can turn explosive very quickly.

**Outbreak** — The infected are loose. More arrive as the raid continues, so do not mistake a cleared room for safety.

**Geared** — The opposition comes heavily equipped with powerful rifles, protective armor and dangerous ammunition.

**Guarded** — Boss guards fill the raid. Expect a mixture of guard equipment and fighting styles.

**Overloaded** — Scavs and PMCs bring high-capacity primary weapons and oversized pistol magazines. Expect long bursts and fewer reloads.

**Matrix Glitch** — Something looks familiar. Groups of fighters share the same clothing, making the raid feel strangely repetitive.

**No Safe Space** — Everyone is looking for you. Combatants across the map, including bosses, track your position and close in.

**Posers Everywhere** — Killa lookalikes are everywhere. They wear his style of equipment, but the people underneath are ordinary fighters.

**Fake Cult** — The opposition dresses like cultists. The robes are familiar; their usual weapons and fighting styles remain.

**Overrun** — Reinforcement squads replace losses when the population falls too low. Clearing an area will not keep it quiet for long.

**Clone Wars** — The PMCs look suspiciously like you. Your appearance and starting equipment have been copied, so check your targets carefully.

**The Thief** — A thief in a Santa hat is on the move with stolen supplies, money and a chance of bitcoins. Catch him before his trail goes cold.

**Smokers Lobby** — The opposition keeps throwing smoke throughout the raid. Expect shifting clouds, poor visibility and uneasy footsteps nearby.

**Time Warp** — Day and night race past while the raid countdown stays normal. Watch your surroundings change through repeated daylight cycles.

**Schizophrenia** — Voices, figures, strange sounds and impossible sights intrude on the raid. Not everything you see or hear can be trusted.

**Gun Game** — Start with a pistol and earn a new weapon with each qualifying kill, ending with rocket and grenade launchers. Finish the ladder for a bitcoin, with a hidden bonus to discover. Your original weapons return by trader mail after the raid.

**Gun Game [Respawns]** — Gun Game with another chance after defeat. Respawn at full health with a pistol and restart the ladder and knife bonus. The raid timer keeps running. Your original weapons return by trader mail after the raid.

**Stormtide** — A violent storm rolls in. Daylight fades within five minutes, heavy rain masks other sounds, and lightning breaks the darkness.

**AK50** — Everyone joins the raid with an AK-50, including you. Your original weapons return by trader mail after the raid.

**Grenadier** — Scavs and PMCs carry plentiful explosive grenades and use them often. Keep your escape route clear.

**Lab Work Day** — Unarmed staff travel between Labs office hubs, with varied walking/running, short rests and occasional nearby-worker chatter while fighters battle around them. Keyed rooms are open and power can fail. Killing staff costs raid experience; three staff deaths call in hostile Raiders.

**Blue Day** — An early air-raid siren warns of incoming mortar bombardments. Strikes return at unpredictable intervals. Find overhead cover when the shells start falling.

**Snowball Trials** — Face the Snowball bosses one at a time as they hunt you. Defeat the whole roster in one life, and keep an eye out for a hidden challenge. You have twice the normal raid time.

**Trials** — Survive ten waves of five hunters. Equipment and opposition grow stronger, and the next wave waits until every current opponent is dead. The final wave carries a bitcoin. You have twice the normal raid time.

**Extreme Trials** — Face ten waves of five identical bosses, ending with the Labyrinth variants. Clear every boss to advance. At least one member of each wave carries ten bitcoins. You have twice the normal raid time.

**Guard Trial** — Fight successive squads of five boss guards. The final wave mixes guards from the earlier squads. You have twice the normal raid time.

**Total Trial** — Take on the full twenty-wave challenge: one hundred hunters, five at a time. Clear each squad before the next arrives. You have twice the normal raid time.

**Spring** — Raid in spring scenery: fresh growth, damp ground and the end of winter. The season lasts for this raid only.

**Summer** — Raid in summer scenery, with full foliage and the familiar green landscape. The season lasts for this raid only.

**Autumn** — Raid among autumn colors, thinning trees and fallen leaves. The season lasts for this raid only.

**Winter** — Raid in winter, with snow-covered scenery where the map supports it. The season lasts for this raid only.

**Wrong Door** — An ordinary doorway may lead somewhere entirely different. Crossing an open door can send you to another doorway across the map.

**Infrastructure Decay** — The electrical grid is failing. Day and night raids can suffer brief flickers and blackouts, with a higher chance at night. Powered buttons and switches stay unavailable until power returns.
